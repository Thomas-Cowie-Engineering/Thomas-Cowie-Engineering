# 💫 About Me:
Hello, I'm Thomas Cowie, a passionate Electrical and Electronic Engineering student at the Russell Group university, University of Sheffield.<br><br>I'm a microelectronics and chip design enthusiast, building, testing and learning every day. I spend my days writing Verilog, diving into RTL design and synthesis, cocotb and Verilog testing, prototyping on FPGAs and taping out on TinyTapeout. <br>Aside from my engineering interests, I'm devoted to learning the German language to fluency. Over the past couple of months I took part in the University of Sheffield's Languages for All programme and achieved an A1 level in German. Ich kann ein bisschen Deutsch sprechen.<br>
